Course overview

Develop a practical foundation in grooming that puts comfort and safety first. Understand different coat types, choose appropriate tools and build a gentle routine for healthy skin, clean coats and happier pets.

  • Recognise coat types and choose suitable tools
  • Brush, detangle and bathe with gentle handling

  • Spot skin concerns and know when to refer to a vet
