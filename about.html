Our Story

Pawsitive Pet Academy was established in 2023 by Sarah Smith in Durban, South Africa.

The academy offers training programmes and short courses for pet owners, animal lovers and people interested in the pet care industry.

Our Mission

To encourage responsible pet care and support learning in animal training and care.

Care

We value the wellbeing and proper care of animals.

Trust

We aim to build trust through a welcoming experience.

Professionalism

We encourage responsible and professional standards.
