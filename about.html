About Pawsitive Pet Academy

Better care begins with understanding.

A warm welcome to a learning space built around the bond between animals and people.

Our story

For pet people, by pet people.

Pawsitive Pet Academy brings pet care and learning together. Our purpose is simple: to make thoughtful, practical education accessible to people who want to do more for the animals in their lives.

Whether you’re welcoming your first puppy or exploring a future in pet services, we help you connect knowledge with everyday action — always with care at the centre.

What guides us

Our mission. Our values.

Help people build the knowledge and confidence to care for pets thoughtfully.

Care

Animal welfare comes first. We encourage patient, kind methods that respect the individual pet.

Trust

Clear information and honest expectations help learners make informed choices at every step.

Professionalism

Thoughtful preparation, responsible practice and respectful communication shape how we work.
