Pet First Aid

Course overview

Build awareness of common pet emergencies and the first steps to take while seeking veterinary help. Learn safe handling, basic first-aid preparation and how to share useful information with your vet. First aid is not a substitute for veterinary treatment.

What you’ll learn

  • Recognise urgent warning signs in dogs and cats
  • Prepare and maintain a pet first-aid kit
  • Handle an injured pet safely and calmly
  • Understand basic wound care and emergency response
  • Arrange safe transport and communicate with a vet

Who this is for

Pet owners, walkers and everyday carers
